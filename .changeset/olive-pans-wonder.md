"marginalia": patch
---

Base reading reports on the whole reading instead of a fragment of it. The report assistant now sees how large each conversation is, delegates long ones to a helper that reads them end to end, and can go back for your exact words on anything the helper flags. Reports no longer stop short because the assistant ran out of steps mid-conversation, and long Chinese conversations are no longer cut roughly four times shorter than English ones. Report generation also stops competing with background summaries for your configured concurrency.
