Add PDF support: import PDF books (drag & drop or file picker) and read them alongside ePubs. PDFs render with crisp canvas pages, fit-to-width with zoom steps, dark-mode inversion, and reading-progress restore. Outlines (bookmarks) become the chapter list, chapter and whole-book AI summaries work on text-based PDFs, and the first page becomes the library cover. Scanned PDFs (no text layer) are detected at import — they remain fully readable while AI summary features are clearly disabled instead of failing silently.
