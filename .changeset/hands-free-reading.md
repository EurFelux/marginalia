Auto-scroll in the reader. Click the new double-chevron button in the reader header to scroll the book at a steady pace, teleprompter-style, for hands-free reading. A floating bar lets you pause, resume, stop, and change the speed from 0.1× to 2.0× (remembered across sessions). Auto-scroll pauses as soon as you scroll, click, or press a key in the text, jump to a chapter, annotation, or search result, or resize the window enough to reflow the text; it stops by itself at the end of the book and when you start reading aloud. Works for ePub and PDF.
