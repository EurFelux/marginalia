Add a library AI companion: a floating assistant in the library lets you chat with your AI assistant about your whole collection, not just one open book. It can browse your catalog, summaries, notes, highlights, and reading stats through tools — and, grounded in its memory of you, discuss your shelf and recommend what to read next. The same assistant panel is reused everywhere (including its conversation list and web-search toggle); conversations are no longer tied to a single book.
