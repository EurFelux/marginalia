You can now customize the AI actions on the toolbar that appears when you select text. A new Selection page in Settings lists them: add your own with a label, an icon and a prompt, drag to reorder, hide the ones you don't use, and edit the prompts of the built-in Ask AI, Explain, Translate and Summarize (leave a prompt empty to use the default). Turn on "Send directly" for an action to send it to the AI in one click instead of filling the message box; anything you were already typing is kept. The first four actions show on the toolbar and the rest go under More. Turn off "Show labels on toolbar" for a compact icons-only toolbar that fits eight actions. In Chinese, "AI 问" is now called "询问". Changes save automatically.
