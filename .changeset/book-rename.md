You can now edit a book's title and author right from the library: right-click a book cover and choose "Edit details". This finally gives you a way to clean up messy metadata from repackaged files (site-suffixed titles, garbled authors) or name books that imported without a title — no more deleting and re-importing. Leaving the author field empty shows the book as "Unknown author"; titles can't be empty. Changes show up immediately on the library card and inside the reader.
