PDF reading is now fully interactive. Select text right on the page to ask the AI about it — with surrounding context included automatically — or to create highlights and notes, just like in ePubs. Highlights draw as crisp overlays that survive restarts and zoom changes, click one to recolor, annotate, or delete it, and the sidebar list shows every annotation with its chapter and page number for one-click navigation. The AI also gains a page-level reading tool for PDFs: it can read any page by number (and, with vision-capable providers, look at the rendered page image — figures, tables, and even scanned books). Chapter navigation jumps to the right page, the current chapter tracks your scrolling, and untitled PDFs now take their title from the file name instead of showing up blank.
