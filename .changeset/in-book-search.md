Search inside the book you're reading. Press ⌘F (Ctrl+F on Windows/Linux) anywhere in the reader to open the new Search tab in the sidebar: matches are listed by chapter (or page) with surrounding text, and clicking one — or pressing Enter / Shift+Enter — jumps straight to it with the match highlighted in the text. Search ignores case, treats full-width and half-width characters alike, finds Chinese phrases across line breaks, and covers text in tables and other places the AI's reading tools skip. Works for ePub and PDFs with a text layer; scanned PDFs say why they can't be searched.
