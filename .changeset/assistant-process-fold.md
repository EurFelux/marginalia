AI replies now put the answer first. Everything the AI did before answering (reading pages and chapters, searching, running commands, and what it said along the way) is folded into a single line above the answer that shows how long the reply took, such as "Worked for 12s". Click the line to see every step. While the AI is working, the line shows the step in progress. When the AI saves a memory after answering, the answer stays visible, and memory steps now read in plain words ("Saving memory …") instead of tool names. A command waiting for your approval is always shown in full. Copy now copies only the answer. Replies from before this update show "Show steps" instead of a duration.
