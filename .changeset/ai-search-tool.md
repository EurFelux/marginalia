The AI assistant can now search the full text of the book you're reading. Ask something like "where does the book talk about X" and it looks it up with a new search tool instead of paging through chapters: each hit comes back with a snippet in its chapter, and the assistant reads on from the exact spot in the text to answer. Search ignores case and treats full-width and half-width characters alike, and works for ePub and PDFs with a text layer.
